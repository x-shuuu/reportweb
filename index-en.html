At the interface,
we rewrite
battery limits.

We study electrochemical energy storage materials and interfaces, focusing on all-solid-state batteries, high-energy cathodes, and advanced characterization. Starting from atomic-scale interfacial structures, we seek to understand how ions migrate, why interfaces fail, and how to turn those answers into devices that cycle.

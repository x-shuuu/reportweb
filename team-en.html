Team

The lab brings together early-career faculty, postdocs, and graduate students from materials, physics, and chemistry. We prefer to understand the problem clearly before deciding which material to make.

WL

Principal Investigator

Longlong Wang Lab Director / PI

Based at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, Longlong Wang works on battery materials and interfaces, including ion transport and failure mechanisms in all-solid-state batteries and the design of high-energy cathodes. Google Scholar lists 3,981 citations, h-index 21, and i10-index 23 (November 2026).

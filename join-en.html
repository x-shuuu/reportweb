Open Positions

Openings

For candidates in materials, chemistry, physics, and related fields, we recruit Ph.D. students, master's students, joint-training students, and research assistants. Research covers all-solid-state batteries, high-energy cathodes, advanced characterization, and theory.

01

Ph.D. Students

1–2 per year

Background in materials, chemistry, or physics; diligent, rigorous, responsible, and collaborative. Strong candidates may apply at any time through the Fast-Track Program (page in Chinese), with monthly assessment for pre-admission and the option to start research on campus early.

02

Master's Students

2–3 per year

Background in materials, chemistry, or physics; genuinely interested in research, rigorous and responsible, with good teamwork and communication skills.

03

Joint Training / Research Assistants

4–5 per year

Background in materials, chemistry, or physics; solid fundamentals, with preference given to applicants with strong grades and research output.

What We Offer

Beyond national and university scholarships, we provide research assistantships and awards for high-impact papers; at least one domestic or international conference per year, support for collaboration visits, and recommendations for further study after graduation.
